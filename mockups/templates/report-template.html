Status migrainosus vs. MOH — surfaced, not decided

The current continuous episode (Aug 3–ongoing) is a candidate for a status-migrainosus reading under ICHD-3 1.4.1. ICHD-3 notes status migrainosus “may often be caused by medication overuse.” Because MOH_GENERAL_CRITERION and a per-class threshold are both met for this reporting window, ICHD-3 directs coding MOH plus the underlying migraine type rather than status migrainosus — that clinical determination is surfaced here for the prescriber, not decided by this system.

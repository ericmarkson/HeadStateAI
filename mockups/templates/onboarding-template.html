Step 1 of 6

Collection consent, on its own screen, before the first question is asked. The baseline conversation on the next step is collection — so it cannot come first. Allow and decline are the same size, the same weight, and neither is pre-selected or focused (FR-7.3).

CollectingStep 2 of 6

What I’ll keep

Two things, decided separately. Neither is bundled into the other.

Your symptoms, medications and timing collect_symptom_data

The record itself: what you log, when it happened, and when you logged it — kept as two separate timestamps, because a Tuesday migraine written down on Thursday is still a Tuesday migraine.

If you decline: there is nothing to keep, so I’ll close without creating an account. This is the one thing HeadState can’t work around.

Coarse location collect_location

City-level only, and only so a day can carry the weather and barometric pressure that went with it. No precise position, no history of where you’ve been.

If you decline: every other part of the app is unchanged. Logging, the recall summary, headache days, migraine days, medication-overuse counting — all identical. A day just won’t have weather next to it.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy General Privacy Policy Two separate documents, two separate routes. The wording above is drawn from the same versioned policy text as the first one.
◆ Separate opt-in for collection, separate authorisation for sharing. Washington’s My Health My Data Act, enforceable by private action — and no single control may satisfy both (FR-7.4). Each Allow writes one append-only consent_events row and updates consent_current in the same transaction.

SharingStep 4 of 6

What can leave

Three ways data can go outward. Each is its own decision, and each is off until you turn it on.

Log through Claude, ChatGPT or Siri share_assistant_connector

Lets you log by talking to an assistant you already use. Your words, and whatever the record sends back, travel through that company’s servers. Whether they keep any of it is undocumented, and I’m not going to tell you it isn’t. Granted one assistant at a time, never all at once.

If you decline: voice and text logging inside HeadState is untouched, including offline.

Send the summary as a link share_export_link

A single-use web link for a clinic that won’t take a PDF. Expires in 24 hours, has a view limit, and every open is logged — including the ones that fail. Revoking it stops future opens; it can’t retract a page someone has already read, downloaded or screenshotted.

If you decline: you can still print the summary, save it as a PDF, or hand over your phone. The recall page itself is unaffected.

Write migraine days back to Apple Health share_health_platform

Pushes your day classifications outward into the Health app, where other apps you’ve authorised can see them. This is data going out. Reading your sleep in is a different ask, on the next screen.

If you decline: nothing else changes, and reading sleep in still works if you allow it separately.

◆ Two scopes are deliberately absent. research_aggregate is not asked at first run — it belongs in Settings, after the product has earned the question. sale exists in the schema and renders no control at all, here or anywhere, pending counsel.

Step 4 of 6

Device permissions, not consent scopes — and each one is pitched as a question I’ll stop asking you, not a box to tick. The weather row is live: go back to step 2, decline coarse location, and the operating-system prompt is never even reached.

PermissionsStep 5 of 6

Three things I can stop asking you

Connect your health data so I can stop asking about sleep.

Reads sleep and rest hours from the Health app. Sleep is the single most tedious thing to type in every morning, and the one most likely to be wrong from memory.

Let me knock once each morning, so a week doesn’t get rebuilt from memory.

One notification, in the morning only — never in the evening. An evening question can’t see the one thing that matters most: whether you slept with a migraine and woke up without one.

Pull the weather so I can stop asking about pressure changes.

Attaches barometric pressure and conditions to each day, from your city. Whether that turns out to mean anything for you is a question for later — it is not a forecast, and there is no risk score anywhere in this app.

You declined coarse location on step 2, so there is nothing to ask the operating system for. Change it in Settings and this comes back.

◆ A consent scope gates the system prompt, not the other way round. Declining collect_location on step 2 means the location permission dialog is never shown — a permission you can’t lawfully use should never be requested.

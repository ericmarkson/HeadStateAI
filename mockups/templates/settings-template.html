What I keep

Two decisions, kept separate — the split FR-7.4 requires, not merged into one settings list.

Your symptoms, medications and timing collect_symptom_data

The record itself: what you log, when it happened, and when you logged it — kept as two separate timestamps, because a Tuesday migraine written down on Thursday is still a Tuesday migraine.

If you decline: there is nothing left to keep — this is the one thing HeadState cannot work around. Withdrawing it now, after your account already holds a record, means deleting the account outright. That deletion flow is not built yet — tech-spec.md §7.20 records the schema for it (deletion_requested_at, per-user key wrapping), not the working path. Declining below only tells you that, honestly, rather than pretending to withdraw something it cannot yet withdraw.

Coarse location collect_location

City-level only, and only so a day can carry the weather and barometric pressure that went with it. No precise position, no history of where you’ve been.

If you decline: every other part of the app is unchanged. Logging, the recall summary, headache days, migraine days, medication-overuse counting — all identical. A day just won’t have weather next to it.

What can leave

Four ways data can go outward. Declining any or all of them leaves logging, recall and day classification fully working (FR-7.5).

Log through Claude, ChatGPT or Siri share_assistant_connector

Lets you log by talking to an assistant you already use. Your words, and whatever the record sends back, travel through that company’s servers. Whether they keep any of it is undocumented, and I’m not going to tell you it isn’t. Granted one assistant at a time, never all at once — consent is checked per platform (FR-6.2), so allowing this covers Claude only; ChatGPT and Gemini would each need their own grant.

If you decline: voice and text logging inside HeadState is untouched, including offline.

Send the summary as a link share_export_link

A single-use web link for a clinic that won’t take a PDF. Expires in 24 hours by default (selectable up to 72), has a view limit, and every open is logged — including the ones that fail. Revoking a link stops future opens; it can’t retract a page someone has already read, downloaded or screenshotted.

If you decline: you can still print the summary, save it as a PDF, or hand over your phone. The recall page itself is unaffected. Any links already created stay listed below, but no new ones can be made.

Write migraine days back to Apple Health share_health_platform

Pushes your day classifications outward into the Health app, where other apps you’ve authorised can see them. This is data going out. Reading your sleep in is the separate integration above.

If you decline: nothing else changes, and reading sleep in still works if it is allowed above.

Let de-identified patterns help check HeadState’s own rules research_aggregate

Lets de-identified, aggregated patterns from your record — day classifications, and which of the algorithm’s proposals you accepted or overrode — be pooled to check whether HeadState’s clinical rules (episode boundaries, day-count thresholds) hold up in practice. Never your free-text notes, never a verbatim utterance. This is a research-and-development use, not enrollment in a study, and it is not the same as sale — that is a separate, unimplemented control pending legal review, and allowing this does not touch it.

If you decline: nothing changes about your own logging, recall or day counts — this scope only controls whether your data contributes to the aggregate pool used to check the rules themselves.

◆ New here, not on the onboarding six-screen flow. Onboarding said research_aggregate belongs in Settings, after the product has earned the question — this is that screen. sale exists in the schema and renders no control at all, here or anywhere, pending counsel.

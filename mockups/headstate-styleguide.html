05

Confidence-tag vocabulary

Closed list — a mockup may not render an eighth value. Each tag lives inside a Card/Receipt field row.

lexicalExplicit match in the transcript ("8 out of 10")
paraphraseMapped through a vocabulary ("really bad" → severe)
userCorrected by the user, superseding an extracted value
no sourceask_status: not_asked — nothing to tag
resolvedA deterministic lookup, not stated directly — still traces to something said
systemNo user utterance at all (e.g. recorded_at) — never correctable
reducedConfidence lowered by BACKFILL_WINDOW — always paired with backfilled copy
Full rationale, token block and the roadmap live in design-system.md. decision-log.md D15–D19
